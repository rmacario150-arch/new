BLE Inspector

Look inside a Bluetooth Low Energy device you own, or watch how close it is.

Your browser shows a list of nearby devices. Pick one of your own. Nothing is sent anywhere: this page talks to the device directly, and reads only.

Custom service UUIDs (for your own projects)

Browsers only reveal services you ask for ahead of time. Common standard services are included automatically. If you built the device yourself (an ESP32 sketch, for example), add its service UUIDs here, separated by commas, before you choose the device.

Pick one of your own devices. The page listens for its Bluetooth broadcasts and shows how strong the signal is. A stronger signal usually means the device is closer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

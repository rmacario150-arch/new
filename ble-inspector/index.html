BLE Inspector

Your Bluetooth toolkit

Checking

Your browser shows a list of nearby devices. Pick one of your own. Nothing is sent anywhere: this page talks to the device directly, and reads only.

Custom service UUIDs

Browsers only reveal services you ask for ahead of time. Common standard services are included automatically. If you built the device yourself (an ESP32 sketch, for example), add its service UUIDs here, separated by commas, before you choose the device.

Pick one of your own devices. The page listens for its Bluetooth broadcasts and shows how strong the signal is. A stronger signal usually means the device is closer. You can add several devices, and each gets its own card. Calibration is remembered on this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Treat this as hot and cold, not a ruler. Walls, bags, and bodies change the signal. For a better distance guess, hold a device about 1 m away and tap "Calibrate at 1 m" on its card. Sound follows one device at a time.

If readings keep stopping, try another mode. Each card shows what is being used.
